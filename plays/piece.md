# A portfolio piece or video

Day 1, think:
- Interview them one question at a time until you can say what they actually care about in this piece.
- Get 10 angles before judging any, then break the box.
- They pick the angle that makes them lean forward and check it against `you/THREAD.md`.
- A 1-minute voice memo of it, no script.

Day 2, make:
- Script from the voice memo, in their words.
- Film it rough. Rough is the goal until the last two days.
- Read it as a reviewer who's seen hundreds today: where would they stop paying attention? Ask how they'd fix it.

Rules that hold for any video:
- Get them into their own story in the first seconds.
- Make it something a slideshow couldn't do.
- Captions on, under the time limit, everything uploaded (reviewers often won't open links).
- Write down their role and the tools they used while they make it, for the description later.
- Credit everyone who helped.
