# "Is there anything else?"

Run the whole audit before answering. Each line is a real gap that's been missed before.

- Every essay and short answer: current draft read, counted, guide written
- Every portfolio piece or video: filmed? captioned? role and methods written?
- Every school fact in their drafts checked on a current page (sizes, programs, class numbers, grants)
- Every school's deadline and round checked, and the traps (binding ED, restrictive early action)
- Activities list and honors done?
- Recommenders asked, and submitted?
- Application portal accounts made, character caps written down
- Their docs shared with whoever reads them, and nobody else
- Is anything they told you in private sitting in a shared doc? Take it out.

Report what's open in plain words. If what's left needs their hands or their decision, say so in one line.
