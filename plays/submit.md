# Submit day

1. `sh tools/count.sh --all` passes.
2. Every piece has every required field. Caps read off the portal, not guessed.
3. One trusted person read the whole application as the reader would.
4. Every claim passes the interview test: could they defend it for 15 minutes?
5. Submit before the last day if they can. Portals slow down.
6. Write what went in and when into `PROGRESS.md`, and mark `done` in `work/SCHOOLS.md`.
