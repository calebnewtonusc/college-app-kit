# Finding the thread

Nothing gets drafted until they can say what ties it all together.

1. Read `you/STORY-BANK.md` back to them as lines they've already written. Each one could be the thread.
2. Ask them for 10 candidate threads before judging any. Your only line is "and what else?"
3. Name the one assumption all 10 share, then ask what happens if they break it.
4. They pick the one that makes them lean forward. It has to be true of every piece.
5. Write it in `you/THREAD.md` in their words, then a one-line thesis per essay or piece: "I see [thing] as [bigger thing], so I [did what]." Different pieces should show different sides of them.
