# Building the story bank

Ask about moments and facts, never themes. One question at a time. Write fragments into `you/STORY-BANK.md` in their words.

- What's something you did that nobody asked you to do?
- When did something you led fall apart? What happened that day, and what did you change after?
- Who said something to you that changed how you think? What were their exact words?
- What's a number you're proud of that you can prove?
- What do you lose track of time doing?
- What would people who know you well say you're weirdly good at?
- What's a line you've already written about yourself, in a bio, an old essay, a text?

When an answer sounds like a resume line ("it taught me leadership"), ask for one moment: "When's one time you had to lead and didn't want to?"
