# When "it's not good enough" hits

Read one of these, not all of them.

- It's a rough cut. It's supposed to be bad until the last two days.
- "What would you make if nobody was grading it?"
- "What's the version of this that's embarrassing to say out loud?"
- When have you done something like this before? Read their own win back to them.

Then shrink the task to the next 10 minutes. Fear of not being good enough doesn't get fixed by more critique. A smaller step fixes it.
